# Nexus Campaigns — Logo Prompt (Gemini-optimized)

Prompts tuned for **Gemini image generation** (Nano Banana / Imagen 3). Gemini
responds best to descriptive natural-language scenes plus an explicit style and
output spec — not keyword soup. Copy a block below, paste into Gemini, iterate.

**What it represents:** an AI-powered Dungeon Master vault that ingests raw
campaign inspiration and weaves it into a linked, quality-gated knowledge graph.
Two visual ideas drive every prompt: **Nexus** (a network of glowing connected
nodes) and **arcane fantasy** (the D&D / spellbook world the content lives in).

---

## 1. Primary prompt (app icon — recommended)

> A minimalist app icon for "Nexus Campaigns", an AI Dungeon Master toolkit.
> Centerpiece: a glowing arcane nexus rendered as a constellation of small
> luminous nodes connected by thin radiant lines, forming the implied silhouette
> of a 20-sided die (d20). The nodes pulse with magical energy. Color palette:
> deep indigo and obsidian-black background, with amethyst-purple and teal-cyan
> glow for the network lines, and a single warm gold accent node at the center.
> Style: clean modern flat-vector with subtle inner glow, slight depth, crisp
> geometry, no text. Rounded-square icon, centered composition, generous padding,
> works at small sizes. High contrast, sharp edges, premium and mystical feel.

---

## 2. Emblem variant (badge / wordmark companion)

> A circular emblem logo for a fantasy campaign knowledge vault. A central
> open spellbook or rune-etched tome from which thin glowing threads radiate
> outward and connect to orbiting nodes, like a knowledge graph blooming out of
> a grimoire. Arcane circle border with faint geometric runes. Palette: midnight
> blue and violet with cyan and gold light. Flat vector illustration, symmetrical,
> balanced, mystical-tech aesthetic blending fantasy and modern data visualization.
> No text. Transparent background.

---

## 3. Monogram variant ("N" mark)

> A geometric monogram logo of the letter "N" constructed entirely from connected
> network nodes and glowing edges — the negative space and node placement form a
> clean capital N. Magical circuit / constellation styling. Deep purple-to-teal
> gradient glow on a dark background. Minimal, modern, scalable vector logo. No
> serifs from a normal font; the letterform is built from the graph itself.
> Centered, transparent background, no extra text.

---

## 4. Ultra-minimal favicon (16–32px safe)

> A tiny, ultra-simplified logo mark: three or four glowing nodes connected by
> bright lines forming a small d20-like triangular cluster. One gold center node.
> Dark indigo rounded-square background. Flat, bold, high-contrast, readable at
> 16px. No text, no fine detail, no gradients that muddy at small size.

## Style anchors (paste-append to refine any prompt)

| Axis | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Mood | mystical, arcane, premium, intelligent |
| Genre fusion | fantasy + knowledge-graph / data-viz |
| Palette | indigo & obsidian base · amethyst-purple + teal-cyan glow · gold accent |
| Form language | thin radiant lines, luminous nodes, geometric, clean |
| Style | flat modern vector, subtle inner glow, slight depth |
| Composition | centered, symmetrical, generous padding |
| Constraints | **no text**, no busy detail, legible when small |

### Motifs to draw from (pick 1–2, don't stack all)
- d20 / polyhedral die silhouette
- open spellbook or rune-etched tome
- constellation / star-map network
- magical circuit, glyph, or summoning-circle border
- linked nodes = the `[[wikilinks]]` that connect every entity

### Avoid
- literal text or letters spelled out (Gemini renders text poorly)
- generic "AI brain", circuit-board cliché, glowing-blue-hologram look
- photorealism, drop shadows, skeuomorphic clutter
- more than one focal motif — keep it readable at 80×80px

---

## Output spec for Gemini

- **Format:** square, 1:1 aspect ratio
- **Background:** transparent PNG (or solid dark indigo if transparency unsupported)
- **Resolution:** request 1024×1024, downscale to the README's 80×80
- **Deliverable:** single centered mark, no mockup, no surrounding UI

> Iteration tip: generate the icon, then ask Gemini to *"keep the exact
> composition and palette, regenerate as a clean SVG-style flat vector with a
> transparent background and no text."* Refine one variable per turn (palette,
> motif, density) instead of rewriting the whole prompt.
